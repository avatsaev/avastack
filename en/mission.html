Mission

AI sovereignty for businesses and public institutions.

AI has become essential to staying competitive and efficient. Yet relying on third-party AI services puts your security, your control and your compliance at risk. Our mission is to give organizations and public bodies AI they fully own: hosted on their infrastructure, built on open source and independent of any external provider.

The risks of third-party AI

Convenient in the short term, external AI services create dependencies that grow with your usage.

Data exposure

Sensitive data is stored on external servers, exposed to leaks and unauthorized access. Depending on the region and local laws, providers may even use it to train their own models.

Compliance gaps

Services that do not follow local or industry regulations such as GDPR or HIPAA turn every document you send them into a compliance issue.

Loss of control

One-size-fits-all solutions rarely fit your goals, and every change to the provider’s policies becomes a change you have to accept.

Unpredictable costs

Costs climb as your AI needs grow, and new subscription models or data-handling fees can change your budget overnight.

Intellectual property

Entrusting your data and AI operations to a third party puts your know-how and proprietary algorithms at risk.

Public sovereignty

For public bodies, data tied to security or public services may fall under foreign regulations, eroding sovereignty and citizens’ trust.

Our approach: open source, on your infrastructure

Open-source platforms are transparent: you can understand, audit and modify every part of your AI system. We deploy, configure and secure them on your infrastructure so they work together, then train your teams to master them.

OpenWebUI

Chat interface

A complete chat platform where users talk to your models and build their own assistants, knowledge bases (RAG) and prompts, under full admin control.

LiteLLM

Model gateway

A single, standard entry point to every model, public or private, with permissions, budgets and rate limits per team and per user.

vLLM

Private inference

Your own models served on your hardware to a large number of users, without performance bottlenecks.

Langfuse · OpenTelemetry

Observability

Detailed monitoring of usage, performance and cost across every AI system you run.

n8n

Automation

Visual flows that turn complex AI automation ideas into workflows anyone can understand and manage.
